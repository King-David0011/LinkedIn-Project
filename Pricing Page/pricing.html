Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have a plan for more than 1,000 attendees? +

Yes, we offer custom enterprise plans for large events. Please contact our sales team for a quote tailored to your specific needs.

What is a host? +

A host is a user who can schedule, start, and manage webinars or meetings. You can add as many hosts as you need to your workspace.

How does the trial work? +

You can sign up for free and access all features immediately. The free plan has no time limit, but does have usage limits (like session duration).

Do you offer pricing per webinar? +

No, Livestorm is a subscription service. However, you can choose a monthly plan and cancel at any time if you only need it for a specific event.

Do I have a limit on the number of webinars I can host per month? +

No! All our plans, including the free Basic plan, allow you to host an unlimited number of webinars.
